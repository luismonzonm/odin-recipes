Meyer Lemon Pie

Ingredients

  • White Sugar: 1 1/4 cups.
  • Large Eggs: 3x.
  • Lemon Juice: 3 3/4 fluid ounces.
  • Butter: 1/4 cup, melted.
  • Pastry Shell: 1 9 inch, unbaked.

Description:

  1. Preheat the oven:
    Gather all the ingredients, and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Blend:
    Place sugar, eggs and lemon juice in a blender. Blend until smooth for about 3 minutes.
  3. Add melted butter:
    Add the melted butter into the mixture and blend for 30 seconds more.
  4. Transfer lemon filling to pastry shell.
  5. Bake:
    Bake in the preheated oven until filling is just set (30 to 35 minutes). Allow pie to rest until completely set before serving (about 15 minutes more).
